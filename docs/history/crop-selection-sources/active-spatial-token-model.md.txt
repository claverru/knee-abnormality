# Active spatial token model

The active model applies target-specific attention MIL to two token sets: coarse tokens
from a light scout on every whole canvas and fine tokens from ReXNet-200 on crops each label
selects, as described in [crop selection](#crop-selection). ReXNet-200 gives 49 fine tokens
per 224-pixel crop and the scout 361 coarse tokens per 300-pixel canvas, so a study yields
1,176 fine tokens (24 crops) and, at the 64-canvas validation budget, up to 23,104 coarse
tokens before masking. The MIL head uses no global pooling, regional queries, token
interaction or grid reduction. The scorer stays 256→128→12 with its configured dropout,
followed by per-target weighted features and linear heads, all shared by both token sets.

Tokens receive four additive embeddings:

| Embedding | Definition |
|---|---|
| 1D | Sinusoidal middle-slice distance in mm from the acquisition midpoint |
| 2D | Anchor in the augmented canvas, plus the crop's integer offset for fine tokens, normalized independently to each canvas axis; 2→64→256 GELU MLP |
| Plane | Learned Axial, Coronal, Sagittal embeddings |
| Contrast | Learned metadata pairs `(0,0)` and `(1,1)` for fluid sensitivity/fat suppression |

`encode_batch` adds them to the coarse projected features, applies the shared `mil_norm` and
scores and pools the coarse tokens once (step 3 of [crop selection](#crop-selection));
`pool_encoded` does the same for the fine tokens and mixes both pools with a learned per-label
gate (step 7). Plane and contrast lookup buffers derive from configured metadata rather
than slot order. The ReXNet adapter derives stride 32 and origin 0 from its convolutions.
The scout adapter derives origin 0 at strides 16 and 32 from the scout's convolutions in
module order. The [CoAtNet adapter](coatnet-224-spatial-backbone.md) derives stride 32 and
origin 12 using its main convolution branch and attention pooling; other backbone mappings
fail explicitly. Geometry and the coordinate MLP execute in float32 outside autocast;
embeddings are cast to visual-feature dtype.

Tokens carry no patient-coordinate geometry: the model reads neither LPS points nor the study
center, and visual validity alone decides token membership. The per-token points and their
geometry validity were removed with the [attention prior](#retired-attention-prior) on
2026-09-16. Series dropout remains 0.20, restoring one present type when necessary.

## Crop selection

Implemented on 2026-09-15 from the [crop selection plan](../../crop-selection-plan.md), which
owns the original rationale; runs and later revisions are tracked in
[ongoing work](../ongoing.md#crop-selection). The shared-crops recipe below (2026-09-16) scored
0.879626 ([result](../validation/2026-09-16-shared-crops-result.md)), below per-label crops
(0.891703), because its crops cover few slices. It replaced the label-private recipe, whose private fine
pooling and label FiLM are rejected
([result](../validation/2026-09-16-label-private-crops-result.md)). `configs/baseline.yaml`
carries it: 300-pixel canvases at 0.5 mm (150 mm), 224-pixel crops (112 mm,
`data.crops_per_label: 2`) and `model.scout_backbone: mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k`. Crop
selection replaced the full-triplet path, whose `00-baseline` run (0.892763) is the comparison and is
reproduced from its saved configuration and source archive. `encode_batch` runs, in order:

1. Series dropout, study reflection on uint8 canvases, then the existing affine,
   gamma/contrast and noise augmentation on whole canvases, per series.
2. The scout's stride-16 map (48 channels, 19×19) concatenated with its stride-32 map
   (576 channels, 10×10) sampled bilinearly at the stride-16 anchors, projected 624→256 into
   one coarse token per cell. Coarse geometry (canvas 2D anchors and visual validity) uses the
   canvas augmentation and reflection inverses.
3. **The coarse pool, computed once with gradient:** the embeddings, `mil_norm` and the
   attention MLP give raw coarse scores `r`, and one float32 softmax per study and label over
   its valid coarse tokens gives the coarse attention and pool. Selection reads a detached copy
   of that attention; pooling (step 7) reuses the same scores and pool. The shared head applied
   to the coarse pool alone is the draft, logged as `val_draft_macro_auc`.
4. **Value map:** each label's own coarse attention `a_t` per cell
   (`crop_selection.value_map`, the one place to swap the selection signal). Selection is
   independent per label, so the earlier `ŷ_t(1−ŷ_t)·‖w_t‖` factor only added a per-label
   constant to log scores; it was dropped on 2026-09-16 without changing any choice.
5. **Per-label selection:** each label independently takes exactly
   `data.crops_per_label` crops per study. A cell's gain is that label's value summed over its
   14×14 footprint; training adds one Gumbel draw per canvas, cell and label to `log gain`,
   evaluation takes the plain argmax, and each canvas offers its best cell. A label takes its
   best canvas; each further crop takes its best unused canvas on a **plane the label has not
   used yet**, then any unused canvas. Only when no unused canvas is left does it add a second
   cell on a canvas it already cropped: the best selectable cell at least 3 scout cells
   (Chebyshev, 48 px, 24 mm) from its crops there, else its best untaken selectable cell. The
   count stays exact; a crop repeats only in a study with fewer selectable cells than `k`.
   Labels do not compete: several may choose the same canvas at the same or different cells,
   and every choice is still cut and encoded, so a study always has 24 crops in training and
   validation. A selectable cell's crop leaves the canvas by less than one scout stride (6×6
   cells per axis at 300/224), and its anchor lies inside all three channels' valid
   rectangles; a study without selectable cells gets no crops. `encoded["crops"]` keeps each
   crop's choosing label for analysis; nothing downstream reads it.
6. Integer 224-pixel windows of the augmented, unnormalized canvas with top-left
   `anchor − 112`, zero outside the canvas. ReXNet-200's plain `forward_features`, without
   label conditioning, gives 49 fine tokens per crop. Fine geometry composes the integer offset
   with the canvas inverses, and each fine token lands on a coarse cell whose raw coarse score
   is added to its own.
7. **Gated pooling over shared fine tokens:** per study and label, the coarse pool of step 3
   and one softmax over **all** valid fine tokens of the study's 24 crops, mixed as
   `g_t · coarse + (1 − g_t) · fine` with `g_t = sigmoid(·)` of a learned per-label parameter
   initialized to 0 (`g_t = 0.5`). A crop chosen by several labels is encoded and pooled once
   per choice, so every label's softmax sees it with that multiplicity. A study without valid
   fine tokens uses its coarse pool alone. One shared head row per label classifies the mix;
   tying it to the coarse pool keeps that pool a valid classifier on its own (the draft), which
   is why the mix is not replaced by separate coarse and fine classifier rows. BCE on the mixed
   logits is the only loss.

Construction probes ReXNet-200 at the crop size and the scout at the canvas size, and
rejects crops not smaller than canvases or whose half size, backbone stride or backbone
anchor origin is not a multiple of the scout stride; the CoAtNet origin of 12 fails that
check. `scout.backbone.*` trains at the backbone learning rate; the scout projection and the
gate use the head learning rate, the gate without weight decay. FP16 inference
(`use_fixed_backbone_chunks`) pads crops to fixed 64-image chunks. The label-private draft
needed autocast with `cache_enabled=False`, as Lightning runs it, because its no-grad call of
the attention MLP could cache gradient-free half-precision weights; no parameterised module
runs under `no_grad` now. The label-private step peaked at 21.3 GiB allocated and took 0.46 s
at batch 8 on the RTX 3090 (`outputs/profiling/private-crops-gpu`); the shared-crops step has
not been profiled. Launch the fold-0 run with:

```bash
rtk proxy uv run --locked just train shared-crops configs/baseline.yaml 0
```

## Retired token graph

A local kNN message-passing graph over all spatial tokens was implemented on 2026-09-14
and retired on 2026-09-15 because trained models did not use it. It had replaced the
additive 3D embedding, `unknown_3d` and its study-level 3D dropout, which stay removed.
Two fold-0 runs, both measured with the encoding diagnostic:

| Run | Graph setup | Macro AUC | Control | Without graph |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| `token-graph` | Two zero-initialized residual layers before `mil_norm`, head learning rate | 0.892376 | Champion 0.892360 | +0.000007 |
| `graph-forced` | Default output initialization, update after `mil_norm`, learning rate 0.0018 without weight decay | 0.892679 | `00-baseline` 0.892763 | +0.000039 |

The [token-graph result](../validation/2026-09-14-token-graph-result.md) owns the
measurements. The plan and graph-era sources are archived as
[historical token-graph sources](../history/token-graph-sources/README.md); the run
source archives `outputs/token-graph/source_snapshot.zip` and
`outputs/graph-forced/source_snapshot.zip` reproduce each run. Removing the graph
changed the source, spatial-token and preprocessing identities, so every earlier
checkpoint, including `00-baseline` and `attention-prior`, requires its archived sources.

## Retired attention prior

Implemented on 2026-09-15 from the
[position research](../research/position-encoding.md#phase-1-head-only-changes) and retired on
2026-09-16. Its fold-0 run [regressed by 0.002177](../validation/2026-09-15-attention-prior-result.md)
against the matched `00-baseline` control, and its learned priors stayed nearly flat. With
`model.attention_prior: gaussian_1d_2d_3d` it added three per-target log-biases
`-0.5 * sum_k ((q_k - mu_k) / sigma_k)^2` (float32, `mu = 0` and `sigma = 2` initially, head
learning rate without weight decay) to the coarse and fine attention scores before masking:

| Term | Tokens | Input `q` | Parameters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3D | Geometry- and visually valid; others got zero | `(abs(dx), dy, dz)` in 100 mm units, relative to the study's mean valid token point | Per target, `[12, 3]` |
| 1D | Visually valid | `abs(1D position)` in 100 mm units | Per plane and target, `[3, 12]` |
| 2D | Visually valid | 2D embedding input: `abs(x)` for Axial and Coronal, signed `x` for Sagittal, always signed `y` | Per plane and target, `[3, 12, 2]` |

The 3D term was the only reader of each token's patient point, the middle-slice LPS anchor with
reflection `S = diag(−1, 1, 1)` applied to the absolute point, and of its geometry validity.
Retirement removed `attention_prior.py`, `model.attention_prior`, `configs/attention-prior.yaml`,
those per-token points and validity, the collated `patient_maps` and `geometry_valid` tensors
and the diagnostic's prior variants; the [reflection preview](../data-preparation/reflection-geometry-and-previews.md)
computes its own LPS anchors. The measured run's sources are in
`outputs/attention-prior/source_snapshot.zip`, and the last implementation is in
`outputs/label-private-crops/source_snapshot.zip`.

## Five-embedding predecessor

The `spatial-tokens-five-embeddings` run, and every later run up to the champion, also
added a 3D embedding (patient LPS minus the accepted study center, divided by 100 mm,
through a 3→64→256 GELU MLP), used a learned `unknown_3d` vector for missing geometry and
replaced available 3D with it for 0.10 of studies during training. It kept seed 42,
`all_5fold_v3`, 224 pixels, 140 mm, 2× validation density, pretrained initialization,
augmentation, labels, loss, optimizer and ten-epoch budget. Strict determinism remained
disabled as in the incoming recipe; the historical average-pooling reference enabled it,
so this execution difference must accompany later comparisons. The five embeddings and
removal of the regional branch constituted one experiment. It was launched with:

```bash
rtk proxy uv run --locked just train spatial-tokens-five-embeddings
```

The completed run is saved in `outputs/spatial-tokens-five-embeddings/`. It selected
epoch 10 at 0.889506 fold-0 macro AUC and **0.929 public AUC**, later surpassed by
[3dsampling at 0.930](../validation/2026-09-13-3dsampling-validation-comparison.md); see the
[validation comparison](../validation/2026-09-13-spatial-token-validation-comparison.md)
for target changes, uncertainty, learning curves, timing and memory, and the
[submission record](../inference/2026-09-13-spatial-token-submission.md).
Historical checkpoints require their original
source bundles; current source/config identities reject them without migration.
