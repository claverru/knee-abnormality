# Knee-side embedding implementation plan

Planned on 2026-09-14. Implements
[approach 1](docs/research/knee-side-and-location.md#1-add-a-knee-side-embedding):
give every study token optional left/right context before attention.
Implementation was completed on 2026-09-14 and is documented in
[modeling](docs/modeling.md#knee-side-embedding) and the
[production side policy](docs/metadata.md#production-knee-side-policy). Training and
the saved-prediction comparison are complete; see the
[result](docs/validation.md#2026-09-14-knee-side-embedding-result). Side interventions,
submission and promotion remain; tasks are tracked in [ongoing work](docs/ongoing.md).

## Objective and comparison

Determine whether explicit knee side helps the model interpret appearance and
position while tolerating missing or incorrect side information. Compare with
the restored `ea19772` champion: **0.892360168 validation macro AUC / 0.936 public**.
The [eight-approach research](docs/research/knee-side-and-location.md) owns alternatives;
the [metadata investigation](docs/metadata.md#knee-side-coordinate-investigation)
owns measured coverage, agreement and acquisition limitations.

Keep ReXNet-200, Steven v4 with gold overrides, seed 42, `all_5fold_v3` fold 0,
224-pixel/140-mm inputs, sigma 40 mm, reflection 0.5, independent disease heads,
observed-target BCE, optimizer settings, batch 8, 16 workers, ten epochs and
2× validation images. Preserve CoAtNet support without selecting it for this run.
Use a fresh `knee-side-embedding` run and preserve the champion's source bundle,
configuration and checkpoint. The retired coupling loss stays removed.

This experiment adds metadata inference, an embedding and missing-side training
augmentation together. It evaluates that package; one run cannot isolate the
contribution of each part. No image-based side inference, learned side classifier,
canonical coordinate transformation or side-conditioned position MLP is included.

## 1. Establish a deterministic metadata policy

Use the same policy during training, validation and Kaggle inference. Inputs
must be available in test DICOMs. Training reports, disease labels, study IDs as
predictors and manually corrected study-side lookup tables are excluded.
Manufacturer is recorded for diagnosis, not passed to the disease model or used
to select an unvalidated vendor-specific side rule.

Reuse the loader's supported study series before random sampling, series dropout
or pixel preparation. Collect normalized explicit laterality and conservative
description evidence from headers already read during indexing. Promote the
reusable parsing from the existing audit into production preprocessing; the
production loader must not import an adhoc script. Retain the audit's word/context
boundaries, including its rejection of ambiguous suffixes such as `_d`.
Conflicting L/R evidence and explicit bilateral values must remain unresolved.

For coordinate evidence:

1. Use original patient positions and native image orientation, spacing and
   dimensions. Reuse `image_center`; do not substitute the separately estimated
   `study_centers` tensor or centered token coordinates.
2. Within each indexed series, select the first, middle and last records in
   filename order, deduplicating indices for short series. Require valid native
   poses for those records and take the median physical image-center X.
3. Take the median across series so long series receive no extra weight.
   A coordinate assignment requires at least two usable series, a study center
   strictly more than 40 mm from zero, and agreement in sign across all usable
   series centers. Positive X gives candidate left; negative X gives right.
4. Record omitted/invalid series and the center range as diagnostics. Do not
   adopt the additional 40-mm range cutoff from the stricter exploratory audit.
   Different frame-of-reference UIDs do not establish registered anatomy;
   agreement remains an empirical acquisition cue.

Resolve the study using this precedence:

| Evidence | Study-side output |
|---|---|
| Explicit/text evidence contains both sides, reports bilateral anatomy, or disagrees internally | Unknown |
| Qualified coordinate side contradicts any explicit/text side | Unknown |
| One consistent explicit side, with agreeing or unavailable coordinate evidence | That explicit side |
| No explicit side; qualified coordinates, with absent or agreeing description evidence | Coordinate side |
| Everything else, including descriptions alone | Unknown |

Near-zero coordinates are inconclusive; their sign does not veto a consistent
explicit tag. The three model-facing states are `0 = unknown`, `1 = left`,
`2 = right`. Preserve source, reason, center X, contributing series and conflicts
for analysis, separately from the model input. Do not represent agreement rates
or coordinate distance as calibrated probabilities.

This policy is deliberately different from the exploratory 96.85% coverage
arithmetic. It excludes text-only assignments, requires coordinate consensus,
and operates on production-indexed records. Known bad files, supported-series
filtering and evidence from additional headers can also change assignments.
Recompute coverage and explain differences before reporting a production figure.
Do not choose thresholds by maximizing disease-validation AUC.

## 2. Integrate with loading and collation

Add the resolver to `src/knee_abnormality/preprocessing/laterality.py`.
Have `read_header` retain the small normalized side evidence needed by the
resolver, alongside its existing native pose. Aggregate it in `index_series`
and resolve/cache side in `KneeStudyDataset._index_study_geometry`.
Preserve evidence from readable headers even when a record is unsuitable for
geometry; side eligibility and pixel/3D eligibility are separate decisions.

Compute once per study per loader-worker cache, using deterministic metadata
independent of sampled slices, validation density or batch composition.
Do not add another DICOM scan or pixel decode during `__getitem__`, and do not
change image selection to obtain a side. A supported study with unknown side
must still load normally. Worker-local caches may repeat work across workers
or restarts; they are not a cross-process persistent cache.

Return `knee_side` in the item and collate an integer tensor of shape `[B]`.
Keep source-side evidence immutable. Record a study-keyed side manifest with
policy/source identity and reasons in the run artifacts, following the existing
provenance conventions. Worker processes must not concurrently overwrite a
shared manifest. Validation outputs must allow a one-to-one join to its side
manifest without changing submission columns.

## 3. Add an optional embedding before LayerNorm

Add two independently learned vectors of width 256: **512 parameters**.
Unknown contributes a fixed zero vector; there is no learned unknown row.
Initialize both learned vectors to zero using an allocation that consumes no
random numbers, after the existing parameter initialization. This keeps initial
side contributions zero and preserves existing parameter initialization.

Resolve one effective side per study and gather its vector for each image's
tokens. Add the term in `pool_encoded`, alongside the existing terms:

```text
token_i = LayerNorm(
    appearance_i + position_1d_i + position_2d_i + position_3d_i
    + plane_i + contrast_i + knee_side_embedding_study
)
```

Broadcast across the spatial positions without changing token validity or count.
Return the side term through the existing `encode_batch`/`pool_encoded` boundary
so saved-checkpoint diagnostics can change it without rerunning the backbone.
It remains available when the 3D term is invalid or dropped. Target-specific
attention and all twelve classifier vectors and biases remain independent.
The new parameter follows the existing head learning-rate and weight-decay rules.

For the initial experiment use `model.side_dropout: 0.10`. During training,
replace known side with unknown for 10% of known-side studies, once per study
per forward, with the same decision for all its series and tokens. Do not apply
inverted-dropout rescaling. Keep this independent of 3D dropout and disable it
in validation/inference. Use the seeded training RNG and record the probability.
This is a proposed starting value, not an optimized result. Extra stochastic
draws mean complete training trajectories are not claimed bitwise identical.

Use this one architecture on the experiment branch, with no enable flag or
legacy checkpoint fallback. The dropout probability is an intentional experiment
parameter; the side mapping, zero-unknown behavior and metadata policy are fixed
and versioned. Zero contribution does not remove all acquisition/missingness
information, and the network can still misuse known-side context.

## 4. Transform side with reflection

Use the existing `reflected_study` decision in `encode_batch`:
left becomes right, right becomes left, and unknown stays unknown. Apply side
dropout to the effective side after this transformation. Do not draw another
reflection decision, infer side again from centered coordinates, or mutate the
cached source side. Reflection-ineligible studies retain their source side.

Pixels, triplet channels and coordinates continue through the existing reflection
path. Medial/lateral disease targets keep their original identities. Ordinary
affine/intensity augmentation does not change knee side.

## 5. Keep training and inference construction consistent

Update the spatial-token and preprocessing identities to describe the side
policy, state mapping, initialization, reflection and dropout behavior. The new
preprocessing module is already covered by recursive source hashing; include
the policy explicitly in the semantic identity as well.

Record `model.side_dropout` in configuration and update affected constructors,
callers and configuration examples together. Let existing strict identity and
state-dict checks reject incompatible checkpoints. Historical champion inference
uses its archived bundle; do not rewrite checkpoint identities or load old
weights by silently ignoring missing embedding parameters.

Review the shared prediction, validation, export and profiling paths for the
new batch field and model term. Source bundles must include the resolver.
The current Kaggle notebook, two-GPU orchestration, worker count and batch size
remain unchanged. New bundles obtain side through the shared loader/model.

## 6. Verification and experiment evaluation

Before handing the implementation back for training:

- Run repository-permitted static checks: Ruff, formatting, catalog consistency
  when scripts change, source/reference review and documentation links. Do not
  write or run tests or launch optimizer steps/model diagnostics automatically.
- Extend the existing metadata audit to apply the production resolver. Start
  from saved header snapshots; use bounded CPU header-only reads for newly
  required evidence. Record the final all-study and fold-specific left/right/
  unknown counts, source/reason counts, raw manufacturer/model groups, conflicts,
  and agreement against explicit and textual references separately.
- Verify the three example-test studies can be assigned or abstained using the
  same resolver, without reports or a training-side lookup. Reconcile audit and
  loader record-selection differences explicitly. Metadata cannot certify the
  true side of every remaining disagreement or characterize hidden-test coverage.
- Review unknown-side masking, shared per-study dropout, reflection mapping,
  tensor broadcasting and optimizer inclusion. Keep the two backbones supported.
  GPU/model execution remains deferred while the GPU is occupied.

The user launches one fresh training run with the fixed recipe. Select the best
checkpoint by the existing full-fold macro AUC. Compare the 904 saved predictions
with the champion: full-fold/per-target AUC, BCE, both menisci, the OA pair, the
other eight targets, exclusive-positive margins and acquisition/co-occurrence
diagnostics. Add original left/right/unknown and metadata-source strata with
supports; report undefined subgroup AUCs rather than inventing values. Preserve
gold diagnostics separately. These exploratory strata do not replace full-fold
selection or provide independent anatomical labels.

Prepare, but do not automatically run, side interventions in the existing
encoding diagnostic: normal side, all side contributions suppressed, and known
sides swapped. Add a reproducible 1%/5%/10% known-side corruption sweep with seed
42, including corruption concentrated in observed problematic acquisition groups.
Use identical encoded features and recompute pooling; record affected study IDs.
This measures dependence and sensitivity, not the actual side-error rate or the
result of training a model without the embedding. Reflection interventions need
their own consistently transformed inputs when that diagnostic is requested.

Inspect learned vector norms/distance as evidence of use, not proof of useful
anatomy. A gain confined to ranking scanner or side groups is weaker evidence
than improved discrimination within them. A regression under suppressed side
is interpretable only alongside normal-side performance and realistic corruption.
Do not declare a gain irrelevant because its decimal difference is small, or
claim one fold establishes repeatability or public/private ranking.

Runtime overhead is expected to be small because header geometry is reused and
the model adds only a vector lookup/addition. Record observed training/validation
timings when the run becomes available; do not claim measured inference speed
or start a separate GPU profiling task. Champion promotion remains a subsequent
decision using the measured results and, when submitted, leaderboard evidence.

## Implementation order and completion criteria

| Step | Main files/responsibility | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Metadata resolver and audit | `preprocessing/laterality.py`, `preprocessing/dicom.py`, `preprocessing/series.py`, existing metadata audits | One deterministic policy, measured coverage/conflicts and reconciled cohort differences |
| Loader integration | `dataset.py` | Cached source side, `[B]` batch field, stable assignments and normal unknown-side loading |
| Model integration | `model.py`, `spatial_tokens.py`, `config.py`, `configs/baseline.yaml` | Zero-initialized two-row parameter, zero unknown contribution, study-level dropout and correct reflection |
| Provenance and callers | `preprocessing_identity.py`, affected train/export/diagnostic callers | Versioned identities, complete bundles and no silent historical checkpoint migration |
| Handoff and evaluation | Topic docs, script catalogs and existing encoding diagnostic | Static checks, recorded metadata evidence and a reproducible run recipe; GPU work only when requested |

Implementation is complete when the fixed data/model contract is integrated,
metadata evidence and static checks are recorded, and the user has a launchable
configuration. Training completion, GPU diagnostics, submission and promotion
are separate steps. Move durable implementation details into modeling/data
documentation when implemented and remove completed tasks from `ongoing.md`.
