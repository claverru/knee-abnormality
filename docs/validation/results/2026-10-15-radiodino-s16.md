kept as a patch in the campaign folder and its worktree was removed.

## The adapter

A plain ViT exposed as a four-stage backbone: the CLS token and its position embedding dropped, the
14×14 patch tokens as NCHW maps, stages of 1, 2, 5 and 4 blocks, the stride-16 middle read after block
8 and the final read through the ViT norm and a 2×2 average pool (origins 7.5 and 15.5, the ConvNeXt-T
anchors). The final maps equal timm's own blocks exactly.

## What the campaign found

Nineteen scored runs, four invalid. One adoption under the rule: the backbone stage groups warming up
top-down, one epoch apart (×4 +0.0004, ×1 +0.0006). Learning rates from 1.4e-4 to 4e-4 flat or falling
with depth, warm-up shapes, anchors stronger and weaker, the feature-statistics placement, the middle
read at block 10 and a learned final merge all stayed within about ±0.002 at ×4 (stronger anchors
−0.0024, the learned merge −0.0019).

**No defect:** the gap diagnosis confirmed the RadImageNet weights in use (anchor buffers equal the
file), ImageNet normalisation as in the model card, DINO's LayerNorm eps and correct geometry; dropping
the CLS token changes the final patch tokens little (cosine 0.979).

**A third of the gap was the reference:** the MambaVision-T reference trained with the mask decoder's
supervision, removed from main afterwards (9a93087). A decoder-free MambaVision-T on the same fold
(`mamba-silver-fold-2`) scores ×4 0.935160, so RadioDINO's own gap is about 0.0055.

**The rest sits on located structures:** 85% of it on Lateral Meniscus and PF OA (−0.015 each),
Medial OA (−0.010), Lateral OA and Medial Meniscus (about −0.008); the diffuse labels are level. The
model under-fits (training loss falls 0.094 from epoch 2 against 0.114), and its tokens see nothing
finer than a 16-pixel patch, where the convolutional and hierarchical models build their stride-16
maps from stride-4 and stride-8 detail.

**The locality line was level:** a convolutional spatial prior (frozen RadImageNet ResNet-50 stem and
layer1 added to the tokens through gates, ×4 −0.0001, gates opened to at most 0.0007), an overlapping
32×32 patch embedding started from the pretrained kernel (×4 −0.0016, border weights held to about
3.5% of the kernel by the early anchor) and a depthwise convolution in every block's MLP (×4 −0.0000,
×1 +0.0012, gates rising to 0.0085 and still growing). Zero-started side paths stayed small under the
recipe's rates and anchors.
