# 2026-09-16 crop224 result

**A tie with the full-triplet baseline, and the losses are geographic.** One 224-pixel
(112 mm) crop for three of every four kept canvases reached **0.891624** fold-0 macro AUC at
epoch 9, **−0.001139** below `00-baseline` (0.892763) and **+0.004473** above the
[160-pixel crop run](2026-09-15-crop-selection-result.md) (0.887151). Targets at the centre of
the joint gained and targets at the edge of the field of view lost, with fracture alone larger
than the macro gap.

## Setup

- **Recipe:** `configs/baseline.yaml` (removed) as of this run: 300-pixel canvases at 0.5 mm (150 mm),
  **224-pixel crops (112 mm)**, `data.crop_canvas_fraction: 0.75` with
  [at most one crop per canvas](../../../history/crop-selection-investigation.md#recipes),
  `mobilenetv3_small_100.lamb_in1k` scout, ReXNet-200, sigma 40, reflection 0.5, series dropout
  0.2, no attention prior, 10 epochs, batch 8, `prefetch_factor: 1`.
- **Split and seed:** `all_5fold_v3`, fold 0, seed 42; 3,503 training and 904 validation studies.
- **Code:** uncommitted working tree on `meniscus-soft-coupling` (HEAD `ea19772`); each run
  directory holds `source_snapshot.zip`.
- **Runs:** `outputs/training/crop224` trained epochs 0–6 and died with its terminal;
  `outputs/training/crop224-resume` continued from `last.ckpt` through epoch 9 with the same 10-epoch
  schedule, so the learning-rate decay is the one the uninterrupted run would have followed.
- **Cost:** 597 s of training and 43 s of validation prediction per epoch, 4,380 optimizer steps,
  peak 19.3 GiB CUDA. A `profile_training --mode gpu` sweep on the eight heaviest studies peaked at
  21.3 GiB at this fraction; fraction 1.0 ran out of memory on the 24 GiB card.

## Reading

- **Zoom works where the crop looks.** ACL, effusion and synovitis sit at the centre of the joint
  and gain; the 0.5 mm sampling gives them more detail than the champion's 0.625 mm.
- **The losses are at the edge of the 112 mm window.** Fracture can involve the fibular head or the
  metaphyses, Baker's cysts are posterior, patellofemoral OA is anterior and the lateral meniscus is
  peripheral. Centered 112, 80 and 140 mm windows per plane and contrast are rendered in
  `outputs/previews/crop-sizes-20260916/centered-crop-sizes.png`; the placement freedom is only
  16 mm towards the top or left and 24 mm towards the bottom or right, since 224-pixel crops
  leave 6 selectable cells per axis on a 300-pixel canvas.
- **The draft is weak.** `val_draft_macro_auc` ends at 0.633, so the coarse tokens alone barely
  predict. They both steer selection and stand in for everything the crop cuts off, which is
  consistent with the peripheral losses.
- Against the 160-pixel run, every target except medial meniscus and medial OA improves, so the
  earlier gap was resolution and field of view rather than the selector.
