non-convolutional family, trained once on fold 2 of `all_5fold_scanner_v2` (seed 1110484336) with

and its worktree was removed.

## Cost

| | Swin-T | MambaVision-T |
|---|---:|---:|
| Training, twelve epochs | 4,023 s | 3,106 s |
| ×4 prediction, 875 studies | 93 s | 64 s |
| Peak CUDA allocation, batch 8 | 23.4 GB | 12.7 GB |

The 23.4 GB peak leaves no headroom on the 3090. The GPU still ran at P2 and 330–345 W throughout,
so no memory spilled to Windows RAM. **Changing image counts cost nothing:** backbone forward and
backward in BF16, 30 random counts from 150 to 260, gave 1.51 ms per image on unseen counts and
1.50 on repeated ones (ResNet-50: 1.03 and 1.01). Swin has LayerNorm only and no depthwise
convolutions, so it needs no image-count padding. Kaggle T4 inference was not timed.

## Decision

MambaVision-T stays: it is ahead alone (+0.0006) and beside the ResNet-50 (+0.0002), trains 30%
faster in half the memory and has a verified Kaggle T4 profile. Each edge comes from one run. For
Swin-T: it needs only timm, no `mamba_ssm` wheel rebuilt for each Kaggle torch. Its own recipe is
untested. MambaVision's rate and anchors were set for MambaVision's weight scale, 1.5 to 12.7 times
the Refined-RadImageNet ResNet-50's, and Swin-T's weight scale was not measured.

## Implementation (not merged)

Built on branch `swin` off `94868bf`, deleted with its worktree:

- **Stage adapter:** `SwinStages` in `modeling/backbone_stages.py`, keyed on timm's
  `SwinTransformer`. `to_stride_8` runs `patch_embed`, `layers[0]` and `layers[1]`; `deep` runs
  `layers[2]`, then `layers[3]` and `norm`. timm's Swin keeps NHWC maps, so the adapter permutes
  to NCHW after stride 8 and back before `layers[2]`. Widths 192, 384, 768 from `feature_info`;
  the decoder mapping is the `.layer2` entry.
- **Token mapping:** `PatchMerging` is a linear layer over 2×2 neighbours, so the mapping walks
  the 4×4 stride-4 patch convolution, then one 2×2 stride-2 step per merge. The anchors are the
  same as ConvNeXt's: origin 3.5, 7.5 and 15.5 px at strides 8, 16 and 32.
- **Early anchors:** `patch_embed.` and `layers.0.` added to `EARLY_ANCHOR_PREFIXES`.
- **Input size:** fixed at 224 px, since each grid side must divide by the window of 7.

Run: `outputs/training/20261014-182318-swin-t-mamba-recipe` (`--name swin-t-mamba-recipe`).

## Other families considered

Counted on CPU with timm 1.0.26 at 224 px (parameters, GMAC), looking for families beside
convolution (ResNet-50 23.5M / 4.09, ConvNeXt-Tiny 27.8M / 4.45) and MambaVision's hybrid:

| Backbone | Kind | Params | GMAC | Fit |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| DINOv3 ViT-S/16 (`vit_small_patch16_dinov3.lvd1689m`) | Plain ViT, self-supervised | 21.6M | 4.70 | Stride 16 only: needs a simple pyramid for strides 8 and 32 |
| Hiera-T (`hiera_tiny_224.mae_in1k_ft_in1k`) | Hierarchical attention, MAE | 27.1M | 4.90 | Tokens in unrolled order: reroll at the stride-8 split |
| PVTv2-b2 | Spatial-reduction attention | 24.8M | 4.03 | Four NCHW stages |
| DaViT-T | Window plus channel attention | 27.6M | 4.52 | Four stages |

Left out: CAFormer, FastViT, MaxViT and CoAtNet put convolution first and attention late, which
overlaps MambaVision. FocalNet is mostly depthwise convolution. EfficientViT-B2 and Twins are much
smaller (1.5 to 2.9 GMAC). Among Tiny Swins, only the V1 checkpoint has ImageNet-22k weights at
224 px with window 7. The SwinV2 Tiny checkpoints are ImageNet-1k only, and the `ms` ones were
trained at 256 px. No 2D Swin pretrained on MRI was found: RadImageNet released CNNs only, and
Swin UNETR is 3D and CT-pretrained.
