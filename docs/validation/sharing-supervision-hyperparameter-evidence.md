1C's per-target/subgroup comparisons, compartment interventions, public-score
audits, the completed eleven-run 1C hyperparameter search and its sigma/LR
follow-ups. It also records the rejected evidence-mask experiment: removing
originally unmentioned report targets reduced validation AUC by **0.011455**.
These historical results use their original configurations and source archives.

bootstrap and case-mixture sensitivity with the actual submission records.

reviews and representative acquisition headers for all 4,407 studies. OA
severity conflicts and manufacturer-dependent ranking changes are measured
vulnerabilities; neither establishes the cause of Kaggle's hidden ranking.
The [transfer research](../research/medial-lateral-transfer.md) owns the retained
future approaches. No new investigation or experiment is scheduled here.
